# Can AI Replace Programmers?
## Research Notes - Slides 7-9

### 1. Can AI Replace Programmers?

Artificial intellegance is changing software development. AI tools can generate code, identify errors and help programmers with their tasks.

However AI is not something that you can rely on everything in software development branch. So human programmers are still in a need for  understanding complex problems, checking code quality and making important decisions.

According to the 2025 Stack Overflow Developer Survey:
- 84% of respondents use or plan to use AI tools.
- 46% do not trust the accuracy of Artificial Intelligence-generated results.
- 64% do not consider AI a threat for their jobs.

These studies illustrates that AI is some kind of threat for humans, but still in some parts there is still a need for human intelligence.

### 2. Skills Future Programmers Will Need

As Artificial Intelligence becomes more popular, programmers will need to know both technical and human skills.

Important Skills Include:

-**Continuous Learning:** Adapting to the new programming tools.
-**Problem Solving:** Finding solutions to programming problems.
-**Communications:** Adapting to working in a team or a group.
-**Cybersecurity:** Keeping software and user information safe.
