## Project Summary
This project was created for our CEP146 group presentation.

Our topic is "Can AI Replace Programmers?"

The goal of this presentation is to explore how Artificial Intelligence (AI) is changing software development and whether AI could replace human programmers in the future.

Our presentation covers:

What Artificial Intelligence is.

Popular AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok.

How AI helps programmers write code and solve problems.

Benefits and limitations of AI in programming.

Whether AI can replace programmers in the future.

Our group consists of three students, and each member is responsible for researching and presenting a different part of the topic.
