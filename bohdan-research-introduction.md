Hello everyone!

Today, our group will talk about an interesting question: Can Artificial Intelligence replace programmers?

Before we answer this question, let's understand what Artificial Intelligence, or AI, actually is.

AI is technology that allows computers to do tasks that usually require human intelligence.

We already use AI in everyday life.

For example, ChatGPT can answer questions, Siri can understand voice commands, and Google Translate can translate languages.
